## Creative objective

Show that WhatsApp sharing should stay simple, but the invite experience should do more than a PDF. Position Shaadi Nyota as a mobile wedding website link where guests can see the wedding experience, find details, and RSVP from the same place.

## Guardrails

- Do not mention plans or prices.
- Do not use the word "attendance."
- Do not imply RSVP is part of every plan.
- Do not invent customer counts, testimonials, analytics, or instant publishing.
- Use natural Hinglish for an Indian audience.
- Use real product visuals only if product UI is shown.

## Recommended visual direction

Premium editorial carousel, 7 slides, 1080 x 1350.

Visual world:

- Deep maroon, ivory, muted gold, soft beige.
- Clean serif headlines with simple sans-serif support text.
- Wedding-paper texture, subtle gold linework, small ornamental dividers.
- Use real Shaadi Nyota website stills only as proof moments, not AI-redrawn UI.
- Avoid fake phone UI, fake WhatsApp screenshots, fake dashboard graphics, and fake chat bubbles.

Layout idea:

- Slide 1-2: PDF/WhatsApp friction.
- Slide 3-6: Shaadi Nyota link experience + RSVP.
- Slide 7: clear CTA.
