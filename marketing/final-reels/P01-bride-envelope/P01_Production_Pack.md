# P01 — Bride POV: More Than a PDF

## Final concept

Aanya explains why she wanted the wedding invitation to feel like the couple's first wedding glimpse rather than a PDF. The Reel then proves the claim with the real ceremonial envelope opening, invitation content, and guest RSVP flow inside a large handheld phone.

## Approved spoken script

> Main chahti thi ki hamara invite sirf ek PDF na ho—woh hamari shaadi ki pehli jhalak ho. Shaadi Nyota par hamari story, saare functions aur zaroori details ek website mein milti hain. Bas link WhatsApp par share karo, aur guests wahi se RSVP kar sakte hain.

## Approval decisions

- Use natural Hinglish for the Indian audience.
- Use fictional bride, groom, and couple points of view across the campaign; do not rely on a founder presenter.
- Aanya must look directly into the camera from the first frame.
- Do not show labels, character names, lower-thirds, or rectangle overlays on the presenter.
- Remove repeated words at clip joins before assembly.
- The product screen should occupy roughly 68–72% of the Reel height.
- Use real screen recordings only; AI may generate the physical phone scene, never the UI.
- A WhatsApp visual is not required when the narration explains sharing.
- Preserve the genuine guest RSVP sequence.
- Treat the invitation opening as a hero moment. Slow it continuously; never freeze it.
- End-card language should describe the outcome, not look like an interactive app screen.
- “in one click” is editorial text, not a button.
- Remove unnecessary question marks, arrows, circles, icons, or overlapping ornament.

## Rejected patterns

- Side-looking presenter or inconsistent character frame.
- Open-ended script that does not explain story, functions, WhatsApp sharing, and RSVP.
- Invented product interface or AI-redrawn screen recording.
- Phone screen that is too small to read.
- CTA copy such as “Start your preview” for this Reel.
- Button-like CTA elements that cannot actually be clicked in a video.
- A frozen reveal frame that resembles a playback problem.
