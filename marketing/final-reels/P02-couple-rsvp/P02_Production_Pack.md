## Approval decisions

- Use Hinglish for the Indian audience.
- Make RSVP the core problem-solution angle.
- Use a fresh couple, not Aanya and not a founder presenter.
- Replace the earlier three-clip presenter structure because it felt visibly stitched.
- Use one continuous presenter take for the problem setup.
- Put the solution line as voiceover over the real product proof, similar to the successful P01 flow.
- Use the real Palace Door website recording; do not ask AI to recreate any Shaadi Nyota UI.
- Show the opening fully, skip repetitive middle sections, and preserve clear RSVP proof.
- Keep the product display large inside a handheld phone.
- Add a dark inner phone-glass inset so the website video corners do not fight the phone plate edge.
- Use a clean ASCII-only CTA text render to avoid question-mark encoding artifacts.

## Rejected patterns

- Three separate presenter clips for one conversation; continuity changes made the edit feel merged.
- Showing the `Bride_and_groom_speaking_home_202608081933.mp4` visual; continuity checks found likely jumps around 3.38s and 5.58s.
- Letting the website video touch a mismatched curved phone edge.
- Bright green edge artifacts from the phone plate.
- Button-like CTA styling or unnecessary circles/icons.
- Special CTA characters that can render as question marks.
- Fake UI, generated dashboards, fake WhatsApp footage, testimonials, counts, or unsupported claims.
