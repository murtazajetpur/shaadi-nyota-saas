# P03 Production Pack - PDF vs Wedding Website Carousel

## Objective

Position Shaadi Nyota as a better alternative to sending a static wedding PDF: one WhatsApp-friendly invite link where guests can experience the wedding website, see details, and RSVP.

## Audience takeaway

Your invite can feel beautiful for guests and still make RSVP/details easier to manage.

## Format

- Instagram carousel
- 5 slides
- English only
- Final upload files: `posting-ready-1080x1350/P03_Carousel_01.png` through `posting-ready-1080x1350/P03_Carousel_05.png`

## Approved slide sequence

1. PDF vs wedding website hook
2. Problem: PDFs create follow-up questions
3. Solution: one invite link for invite, details, and RSVP
4. Product proof: envelope-opening mobile invite
5. CTA: send one link, guests experience and RSVP
