# P03 Carousel Copy Draft v3 - 5 Slides, English Only

## Creative direction

- Format: Instagram carousel, 5 slides maximum.
- Language: English only. Do not use Hindi, Hinglish, Devanagari, or transliterated Hindi words.
- Core message: A wedding invite should be more than a PDF. Shaadi Nyota gives guests a beautiful mobile invite experience, WhatsApp-friendly sharing, all details in one place, and RSVP from the same link.
- Product proof: Use the envelope-opening Shaadi Nyota experience, not the palace-door screenshot.
- Claim guardrails: Do not invent WhatsApp screenshots, fake dashboards, guest counts, testimonials, delivery analytics, prices, or unsupported plan claims.

---

## Slide 1 - Hook

Kicker: PDF VS WEDDING WEBSITE

Headline: Your wedding invite can do more than a PDF.

Body:
Send one link on WhatsApp. Let guests experience the invite, details, and RSVP in one place.

Visual note:
Show a luxury contrast between a simple PDF card and a richer wedding website experience. Keep it premium, editorial, and uncluttered.
