# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Shaadi Nyota serves couples building and managing a digital wedding invitation, administrators supporting those weddings, and guests opening personalized invitations on mobile devices. Couples need a guided builder that makes complex invitation, event, guest, RSVP, and delivery workflows feel straightforward without exposing implementation details.

## Product Purpose

Shaadi Nyota helps couples create a premium, customizable wedding invitation website, share personalized guest links, manage event-specific invitations, and track RSVP responses. Success means the dashboard is reliable and understandable, previews match the published experience, and guests can use the invitation comfortably on common mobile devices.

## Brand Personality

Premium, warm, and dependable. The invitation experience can be expressive and celebratory; the builder should remain calm, focused, and operational.

## Anti-references

- Theme-locked builders that prevent section-level customization.
- Dashboard interfaces dominated by decorative cards, oversized marketing typography, or unfamiliar controls.
- Forms that expose raw asset paths, database terminology, or implementation details to couples.
- Previews that differ materially from the published invitation.
- Heavy animations or media behavior that compromises mobile performance and accessibility.

## Design Principles

1. Themes provide useful starting defaults, while sections remain independently customizable.
2. Use visual selection and immediate preview feedback instead of requiring technical knowledge.
3. Preserve parity between editor previews and published invitations.
4. Put reliability, data integrity, and clear recovery states ahead of decorative novelty.
5. Design mobile-first for guests and keep dashboard workflows efficient for repeated editing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard accessibility for dashboard controls. Respect reduced-motion preferences, provide meaningful labels and focus states, avoid relying on color alone, and keep touch targets usable on mobile devices.
